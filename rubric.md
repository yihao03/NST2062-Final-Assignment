# Creativity rubric

- originality: how special it is 1: very boring usecase, almost how the thing is intended to work in the first place 3: somewhat interesting idea 5: i wld have never thought of this
- feasibility: 1: totally doable 3: can do but might not work well 5: doesn't work at all
- humour: 1: nothing special 3: lowkey funny 5: ridiculous
- elaboration: this rating should be given in relation to the complexness of the idea: if the idea was simple but well explained, restrict it to 3 points. if the idea is novel and well explained, then 5
